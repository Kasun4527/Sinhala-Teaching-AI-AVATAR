# Abstract V5 — Supervisor Revision (Less Jargon, More Impact)

**Word Count:** ~365 words

---

Sri Lanka faces a critical national shortage of over 30,000 qualified teachers with student-to-teacher ratios exceeding 50:1 [1], limiting educational access for General Certificate of Education Ordinary Level and Advanced Level students [2]. Existing digital platforms provide static, prerecorded content and lack adaptive personalization, while general-purpose Large Language Models lack Sri Lankan curriculum alignment. To address this gap, this final year project presents an Artificial Intelligence (AI) driven Sinhala teaching assistant that adheres strictly to the national syllabus and dynamically adapts to each student's knowledge state. The system integrates a custom Sinhala LLM, a hybrid knowledge-tracing personalization engine, a secure retrieval pipeline, a multi-agent AI architecture, a computer-vision engagement detector, and a 3D avatar teacher.

To ensure pedagogical accuracy, the core language model (SinLlama-8B [3]) was fine-tuned on a custom dataset derived from the national curriculum, enabling it to generate structured, syllabus-aligned Sinhala explanations. A Hybrid Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (Hybrid-BKT) engine combines statistical modeling with deep learning to profile student mastery. This enables the system to perform real-time, per-answer difficulty adjustments while correcting for student guessing. To prevent AI hallucinations, a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline digitizes national textbooks and employs a dual-LLM architecture, ensuring that all lesson generation and Q&A interactions are strictly grounded in verified educational materials. These processes are orchestrated by a multi-agent architecture that coordinates specialized AI agents to govern a continuous, four-phase adaptive teaching lifecycle: pre-assessment, content generation, post-assessment, and continuous adaptation. 

To humanize the digital learning experience, an interactive 3D avatar teacher streams live, delivering lessons with natural voice synthesis and synchronized lip movements. Simultaneously, a Student Engagement Detection Engine utilizes computer vision to analyze facial cues, head pose, and phone usage, allowing the system to monitor attentiveness and detect distraction. Finally, the ecosystem is accessible across web and mobile platforms, providing an interactive learning portal for students, an automated textbook digitization portal for administrators, and a dedicated mobile application for parents to monitor their child's academic progress in real-time.
